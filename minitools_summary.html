Involve Asia · Internal Tool

Mini Tools

Fraud Detection Reporting, Analysis & Case Ticketing — an internal web app for the Compliance & Fraud Prevention team.

Phase 1 — Now
Reads from Mahadir (read-only)
3 Modules · 6 Fraud Categories
Mini Tools reads data from Mahadir — Involve Asia's internal data platform — and presents it in actionable views. It does not store raw transaction data. All data flows are read-only from Mahadir, keeping the source of truth clean and centralised.
The 3 Modules
📊
Module A
Data Reporting
Fraud analysis & case ticket reporting
A1
Overall & Category Analysis Total cases, publishers flagged, revenue at risk — filterable by day, week, month, or custom range. Category-level breakdown per fraud type.
A2
Case Ticket Analytics Cases by period & category, publisher frequency, avg. time from detection to creation, and detection-to-closure SLA tracking.
A3
Offer Approval Support Publisher fraud history lookup for offer approvals — search by Publisher ID, see all-time cases, categories, outcomes, and dates. Shareable via direct link.
🎫
Module B
Case Ticketing
Full lifecycle management of all fraud cases
B1
Ticket Generation S1, S2, and S2+ cases auto-generate tickets. Phase 1: card sent for manual action. Phase 2: auto-applied by enforcement profile.
B2
Auto Categorisation Offer profile (Growth / Balanced / Protection-Focused) pulled from Mahadir. Violation type auto-mapped to fraud category.
B3
Smart Classification Suggests fraud category and risk level based on violation type and revenue impact. Officer can accept or override.
B4
Automated Actions Block promotion, reject conversions, block withdrawals, notify AM. Severity depends on offer profile.
B5
Full Lifecycle Tracking Open → Under Review → Proof Submitted → Proof Accepted/Rejected → Closed. Full audit trail at each stage.
🚫
Module C
Domain Blacklist
Blocking management & click impact reporting
C1
Blocking Overview Central view of current blocklist — domain name, date added, source (Advertiser-Protected / Internal), and active status. Filterable and searchable.
C2
Smart Assist — Weekly List for Kaishen Auto-pulls new domain candidates from Mahadir. Officer reviews, edits, then submits in one click. No manual email needed.
C3
Smart Detect — Trip.com Comparison Weekly rotation: remove bottom-performing internal domains, add top 100 new candidates from Mahadir — after cross-checking against Trip.com's locked list.
C4
Click Impact Reporting Publisher & offer click reports — total, blocked, block rate %. Top 5/10 publishers and offers by blocked volume with mini bar chart.

Enforcement — Inside the Ticket
Every enforcement ticket has a task checklist assigned to the responsible team. The ticket cannot move to Closed until all applicable tasks are ticked. Each tick records who did it and when.
📋 Enforcement Checklist — Publisher #7741
✓
Block Promotion (Platform API) Ops
✓
Notify Account Manager Ops
Block Withdrawal (Zoho) Finance
Reject Conversions (Odoo) Validation
Apply Publisher Ban Ops
🟡 Unblock Checklist — Publisher #3371 (Day 30 reached)
Unblock Promotion (Platform API) Ops
Unblock Withdrawal (Zoho) Finance
Rules
✦ Each task can only be ticked by the assigned team
✦ When all tasks are ticked, card tag auto-changes to 🟢 Ready to Close — user then moves it to Closed
✦ Tasks not applicable to the profile are auto-greyed — not manually skipped
✦ Every tick records name and timestamp — full audit trail
✦ When 30-day timer expires, system auto-moves card from Waiting → Needs Action as Unblock Required and notifies Ops
Enforcement behaviour differs by Offer Profile. The checklist items and monitoring period are determined automatically when the ticket enters Enforcement.

📢 Notify AM & commercial team only
