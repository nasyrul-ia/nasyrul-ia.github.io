Mini Tools

Fraud Detection Reporting, Analysis & Case Ticketing for Involve Asia’s Compliance team.

Prepared By Compliance & Fraud Prevention — Ops Lead
Submitted To Product Team & Engineering Team
Date May 2026
Version 1.0

Mini Tools is an internal web application for Involve Asia’s Compliance and Fraud Prevention team. It supports fraud detection reporting, analysis, and case management across the affiliate network.

The system reads data from Mahadir — Involve Asia’s internal data platform — and presents it in actionable views. Mini Tools does not process or store raw transaction data. All data is sourced read-only from Mahadir.

Module Overview

Fraud detection dashboards, category-level analysis, and case ticket analytics for the compliance team and commercial stakeholders.

A

Data Reporting

Fraud detection analysis and case ticket reporting

The Data Reporting module provides the compliance team and commercial stakeholders with a unified view of fraud detection activity across the network. All data is read from Mahadir.

A1

Overall & Category-Level Fraud Detection Analysis

The dashboard provides a summary of fraud activity across all detection categories, with flexible time filters:

  • Overall fraud detection metrics — total cases detected, publishers flagged, revenue at risk
  • Filter by time period: day, week, month, or custom date range
  • Category-level breakdown — one view per fraud type showing case count, publishers affected, and offers impacted
  • Detection source filter — view cases by S1 (advertiser-reported) or S2 (internally detected)
A2

Case Ticket Analysis

A dedicated reporting view for case management performance:

  • Number of cases generated — by period, fraud category, and detection source
  • Number of publishers involved — with breakdown by category and frequency
  • Average time from detection to ticket creation
  • Average time from ticket creation to case closure
A3

Offer Approval Support

A publisher fraud history lookup to support offer approval decisions. When Ashok is reviewing whether to approve a publisher for an offer, he can search by Publisher ID or name and immediately see:

  • Total fraud cases flagged (all time and by period)
  • Breakdown by fraud category
  • Case outcomes — closed, enforced, or pending
  • Dates of each flagged case
Direct Link Access

This view must be accessible via a direct link so it can be referenced from the offer approval workflow without navigating through the full system.

B

Case Ticketing

Full lifecycle management of all fraud violation cases

The Case Ticketing module manages all fraud cases from the moment they are detected to the moment they are closed. Both S1 (advertiser-reported) and S2 (internally detected) cases are handled through this system.

B1

Ticket Generation

All detected fraud cases — whether reported by an advertiser (S1) or flagged by Mahadir’s internal detection (S2) — will generate a case ticket in the system. In Phase 1, tickets are created manually by the compliance team. In Phase 2, all actions — Block Promotion, Reject Conversions, Block Withdrawals, and Case Creation to Account Manager — will be executed automatically within app.involve without any manual task from the operations or finance team.

S1 Case Rule

If a case originates from S1 (advertiser-reported), the ticket enforcement category is automatically set to Protection-Focused, regardless of the advertiser’s profile setting. This means traffic is suspended immediately, all payouts are blocked, conversions are rejected, and a permanent publisher ban is applied.

B2

Automatic Categorisation

When a ticket is created, the system automatically categorises it based on the offer and the violation type:

  • Offer ID and offer profile (Growth-Focused / Balanced / Protection-Focused) are pulled automatically from Mahadir
  • Violation type is mapped to one of the six fraud categories
  • The advertiser profile determines which enforcement controls apply to that case
B3

Smart Classification

The system analyses each case and suggests the fraud category and risk level based on two factors:

  • Type of violation — matched against detection rule criteria
  • Revenue impact — pending conversion value at the time of flagging

The compliance officer can accept the suggestion or override it. Smart classification uses rule-based logic in Phase 1 and can be upgraded to a machine-learning model in Phase 2.

B4

Automated Actions

When a case moves to enforcement, the system triggers the required downstream actions based on the advertiser profile. Each action connects to the relevant system automatically:

Action What Happens
Block Promotion Publisher’s traffic on the flagged offer is suspended via the Platform API. Applied immediately for Protection-Focused, after proof failure for Balanced.
Reject Conversions A ticket is submitted to the Validation team (via Odoo or email) to reject all flagged conversions for the specified period.
Block Withdrawals A ticket is submitted to the Finance team (via Zoho) to block the publisher’s withdrawal — express, early, and/or standard payouts depending on the profile.
Case Creation to AM An automated notification is sent to the publisher’s Account Manager and Publisher Manager with case details, ticket link, and the proof submission deadline.
Growth-Focused Profile

For Growth-Focused advertiser profiles: no traffic block, no withdrawal block, and no conversion rejection. The only action is to notify the commercial team and Account Manager.

B5

Full Lifecycle Tracking

Every ticket is tracked from creation to closure with a complete audit trail:

Stage 1
Open
Ticket created. Offer, publisher, fraud category, advertiser profile, and revenue at risk captured.
Stage 2
Under Review
Publisher Manager notified. 5-day proof submission window started. SLA countdown active.
Stage 3
Proof Submitted
Publisher uploads proof. Compliance team assigned for review.
Stage 4
Proof Accepted / Rejected
Outcome recorded. Controls lifted (accepted) or enforcement applied (rejected).
Stage 5
Closed
All actions confirmed. Audit log finalised. All parties notified. Dashboard updated.
SLA: 5 Working Days

The SLA deadline is set at 5 working days from ticket creation. The system shows a live countdown on each ticket, sends a warning alert at 3 days remaining, and escalates automatically if the deadline is breached.

C

Domain Blacklist

Domain blocking management, smart list generation, and impact reporting

The Domain Blacklist module manages Involve Asia’s domain blocking operations. It replaces the current manual email-based workflow and provides full visibility into which domains are blocked, how the list is managed, and what impact blocking has on publisher and offer traffic.

C1

Domain Blocking Overview

A central view of the current domain blocklist:

  • Total number of domains currently blocked on the platform
  • Domain list table showing: domain name, date added, source (Advertiser / Internal (Mahadir)), and status (Active / Removed)
  • Filter by category, date range, status, and source
  • Search by domain name
C2

Smart Assist — Weekly Domain List for Kaishen

The system assists the compliance team in preparing and delivering the weekly domain blocklist to Kaishen (Tech team contact for platform-level domain blocking):

  • Automatically pulls new domain candidates from Mahadir — domains flagged but not yet on the blocklist
  • Compliance officer reviews the domain list and can add or remove domains before submission
  • One-click submission sends the finalised domain list to Kaishen through the system — no manual email required
  • Submission history is recorded: date submitted, number of domains, who approved, and Kaishen acknowledgement status
Phase 2 Automation

In Phase 2, this process is fully automated — the system generates and sends the list to Kaishen every Monday morning without manual intervention.

C3

Smart Detect — Trip.com Domain List Comparison

The system distinguishes between two types of domains on the blocklist. Advertiser-protected domains (Trip.com’s list) are permanently locked — they cannot be removed under any circumstance without explicit advertiser approval. Internal domains (sourced from Mahadir) are managed dynamically and can be rotated weekly.

Weekly Rotation Process:

  • Remove: Identify the bottom-performing internal domains — those with the lowest blocked click volume over the past week — and flag them for removal
  • Add: Pull the top 100 new domain candidates from Mahadir based on highest blocked click activity. Before flagging for addition, the system cross-checks against the Trip.com advertiser-protected list and excludes any domain that already appears there. Only domains that are not a subset of the advertiser list are eligible to be added.
  • Submit to Kaishen: The system prepares a weekly change request (domains to remove + domains to add). Kaishen executes the changes on the platform.
Trip.com Domains Are Protected

Trip.com domains are excluded from removal — they are always retained regardless of click volume.

C4

Click Impact Reporting

The module provides reporting on how domain blocking is affecting traffic across publishers and offers:

Report What It Shows
Publisher Click Report Publisher ID, Publisher Name, Total Clicks, Blocked Clicks, Block Rate % — filterable by date range and sortable by block rate.
Offer Click Report Offer ID, Offer Name, Total Clicks, Blocked Clicks, Block Rate % — filterable by date range and sortable by blocked volume.
Top 5 Publishers The five publishers with the highest blocked click volume — displayed as a ranked card with a mini bar chart.
Top 5 Offers The five offers with the highest blocked click volume — displayed as a ranked card with a mini bar chart.

Delivery Approach

Mini Tools is planned in two phases

Phase 1

Manual Assisted — MVP

  • System auto-creates tickets on detection
  • Auto-sends notifications to Account Managers and Publisher Managers
  • Auto-applies enforcement controls on SLA breach
  • Auto-submits weekly domain list to Kaishen
  • Manual review and classification by compliance officers
  • Smart Assist for domain list preparation

Phase 2

Automated Enforcement

  • System auto updates in app.involve — verifying publishers
  • Auto reject affected conversions
  • Auto block withdrawals
  • Auto remove and add domain blocking list
  • ML-powered smart classification replaces rule-based logic
  • Fully automated Kaishen weekly domain submission
